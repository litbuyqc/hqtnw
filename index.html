What is an
Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product research list organized for shoppers using or researching Oopbuy. It may contain seller links, categories, prices, sizes, and notes. Each entry still needs to be checked against its original listing. This page provides a browsing directory and research framework; it does not provide a downloadable spreadsheet or live inventory feed.

01 / DISCOVER

Browse with a purpose.

Choose one category and define what you need: a particular fit, material, color, or use. Oopbuy finds become easier to compare when your shortlist has a clear purpose.

02 / DOCUMENT

Keep the original evidence.

For each entry in your Oopbuy product list, save the original seller URL, item variant, stated price, size information, and the date you checked it.

03 / REVIEW

Compare the whole purchase.

Review measurements, available inspection photos, seller terms, and the full estimated cost. Recheck the original listing before paying because details can change.

Direct answers to common questions about using a spreadsheet for product research.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product research list organized for shoppers using or researching Oopbuy. It may contain seller links, categories, prices, sizes, and notes. Each entry still needs to be checked against its original listing.

Is this an official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent research guide. Its browsing links lead to CAFANS, including the category pages shown in the directory.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No downloadable spreadsheet is provided. This page offers a category directory, a comparison checklist, and a budget planner that you can use alongside your own spreadsheet.

How should I check sizing before ordering?

Compare the exact seller size chart with measurements from a similar item you own. For shoes, check the stated sizing system and any available length measurements. Ask for clarification when measurements are missing.

Can a spreadsheet confirm authenticity or product quality?

No. A spreadsheet entry or reference image cannot verify authenticity, seller authorization, or the quality of a specific item. Check provenance, listing details, and any available inspection evidence independently.

How do I estimate the total shopping cost?

Add the item subtotal, quoted delivery costs, applicable service fees, and any tax or duty estimate. Use one currency throughout. The planner on this page adds your own figures and does not supply live shipping, exchange, or tax rates.
